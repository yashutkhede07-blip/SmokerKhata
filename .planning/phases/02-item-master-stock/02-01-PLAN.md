# Plan 02-01: Item Master, Categories, and Variants Models & APIs

## Objective
Implement `inventory` Django app with `Category`, `Item`, and `ItemVariant` models inheriting from `ShopScopedModel`, `UUIDModel`, `TimeStampedModel`, and `SoftDeleteModel`. Create DRF endpoints with filters for categories, active status, and search.

## Artifacts this phase produces
- Classes: `Category`, `Item`, `ItemVariant`, `CategorySerializer`, `ItemSerializer`, `ItemVariantSerializer`, `CategoryViewSet`, `ItemViewSet`
- Endpoints:
  - `/api/inventory/categories/`
  - `/api/inventory/items/`
  - `/api/inventory/items/{id}/variants/`
