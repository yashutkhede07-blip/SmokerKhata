# Plan 03-02: Supplier Ledger, Purchase Bills, and Payment Tracking

## Objective
Implement supplier master and supplier ledger (`Supplier` and `SupplierLedgerEntry` models). Support recording wholesale purchase bills (`PURCHASE_BILL`) and supplier payments (`PAYMENT_MADE`), computing running balance owed to suppliers in paise.

## Artifacts this phase produces
- Classes: `Supplier`, `SupplierLedgerEntry`, `SupplierSerializer`, `SupplierLedgerEntrySerializer`, `SupplierViewSet`, `SupplierLedgerView`
- Endpoints:
  - `/api/ledgers/suppliers/`
  - `/api/ledgers/suppliers/{id}/entries/`
  - `/api/ledgers/suppliers/{id}/ledger/`
