# Plan 03-01: Customer Udhaar Ledger, Balances, WhatsApp Link & Aging Report

## Objective
Implement customer master and credit ledger (`Customer` and `CustomerLedgerEntry` models). Support credit entries (`CREDIT_GIVEN`), payment entries (`PAYMENT_RECEIVED`), running balance computation in paise, sorting by highest outstanding balance, WhatsApp reminder link generation (`wa.me`), and aging debt reporting.

## Artifacts this phase produces
- Classes: `Customer`, `CustomerLedgerEntry`, `CustomerSerializer`, `CustomerLedgerEntrySerializer`, `CustomerViewSet`, `CustomerLedgerView`, `CustomerAgingReportView`
- Endpoints:
  - `/api/ledgers/customers/`
  - `/api/ledgers/customers/{id}/entries/`
  - `/api/ledgers/customers/{id}/whatsapp-link/`
  - `/api/ledgers/customers/reports/aging/`
