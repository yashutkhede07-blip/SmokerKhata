# Plan 03-03: Ledger Immutability, Tamper Protection & Role Restrictions

## Objective
Enforce immutability and tamper protection across both Customer and Supplier ledgers (cannot alter past credit entries or payments). Enforce shop role permissions (staff/cashier can add credit/payment entries; only owners and managers can add/edit supplier entities).

## Artifacts this phase produces
- Classes: `IsLedgerStaffOrManager`, `IsSupplierManagerOrOwner`
- Tests: `test_ledger_immutability.py`

## Tasks

<task id="03-03-01">
  <read_first>
    - backend/ledgers/models.py
    - backend/accounts/permissions.py
  </read_first>
  <action>
    Enforce model-level save/delete safeguards raising ValueError if any update or delete is attempted on CustomerLedgerEntry or SupplierLedgerEntry.
    Define permission classes in backend/ledgers/permissions.py:
    - Customer create/view & entry addition: IsShopMember (cashiers, managers, owners can record daily udhaar).
    - Supplier creation & modification: IsShopManagerOrOwner (only managers/owners can manage wholesale suppliers).
  </action>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - CustomerLedgerEntry.save() on existing instance raises ValueError
    - CustomerLedgerEntry.delete() raises ValueError
    - SupplierLedgerEntry.save() on existing instance raises ValueError
    - SupplierLedgerEntry.delete() raises ValueError
    - Cashier attempting to create or edit a supplier receives HTTP 403 Forbidden
  </acceptance_criteria>
  <verify>
    <automated>pytest backend/tests/test_ledger_immutability.py</automated>
    <fails_when>non-zero exit</fails_when>
  </verify>
