# Plan 06-02: Reports Computation Core & Aggregations

## Goal
Implement business aggregation queries and analytics reporting core in `backend/reports/`:
- Daily, weekly, monthly sales & profit summaries (Total Sales, Total Cost, Gross Profit) with integer paise precision and lakh display formatting (`REPT-01`).
- Top-selling items report ranked by quantity and revenue (`REPT-02`).
- Item profit margins report calculating absolute margin and percentage (`REPT-03`).
- Support synchronous calculation for short ranges (<7 days) and job dispatch for large ranges (`REPT-06`).

## Requirements Covered
- **REPT-01**: Sales and gross profit summaries (daily, weekly, monthly).
- **REPT-02**: Top-selling items report with quantity and revenue.
- **REPT-03**: Margin per item report.
- **REPT-06**: Range-based handling with background job dispatch for large intervals.

## Tasks
1. Create `backend/reports/` Django app and register in `INSTALLED_APPS`.
2. Implement `ReportCalculationService`:
   - `get_sales_profit_summary(shop, date_from, date_to)`: Aggregates `Bill` and `BillItem` records.
   - `get_top_selling_items(shop, date_from, date_to, limit=10)`: Aggregates items by sold quantity and revenue.
   - `get_item_margins(shop)`: Calculates cost vs selling price margins per item.
3. Expose JSON analytics endpoints:
   - `GET /api/reports/sales-summary/`
   - `GET /api/reports/top-items/`
   - `GET /api/reports/margins/`
4. Enforce role permissions (cashiers denied reports access unless `can_view_reports=True`).

## Verification
- Unit test ensuring gross profit equals total sales minus cost of goods sold.
- Unit test ensuring top-selling items ordering.
