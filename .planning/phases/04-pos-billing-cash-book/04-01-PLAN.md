# Plan 04-01: POS Bill Generation, Offline Sequences & Payment Modes

## Objective
Implement `billing` Django app with `Bill`, `BillItem`, and `DeviceSequenceTracker` models. Support high-speed counter billing with multi-category items, offline-safe unique bill numbering (`{prefix}-{device_id}-{local_sequence}`), payment modes (`CASH`, `UPI`, `UDHAAR`, `SPLIT`), and WhatsApp bill sharing URL (`wa.me`).

## Artifacts this phase produces
- Classes: `Bill`, `BillItem`, `DeviceSequenceTracker`, `BillSerializer`, `BillItemSerializer`, `BillViewSet`
- Endpoints:
  - `/api/billing/bills/`
  - `/api/billing/bills/{id}/whatsapp-link/`
  - `/api/billing/next-sequence/`
