# Plan 04-03: Daily Cash Book, Closing Reconciliation & Discrepancy Tracking

## Objective
Implement `cashbook` Django app with `CashBookDay` and `CashBookEntry` models. Support opening cash entry (CASH-01), ad-hoc cash-in and cash-out entries (CASH-02), dynamic calculation of expected closing cash using POS cash sales (CASH-03), actual physical cash closing entry by manager/owner (CASH-04), and discrepancy recording where mismatches are flagged and never silently auto-corrected (CASH-05).

## Artifacts this phase produces
- Classes: `CashBookDay`, `CashBookEntry`, `CashBookDaySerializer`, `CashBookEntrySerializer`, `CashBookViewSet`
- Endpoints:
  - `/api/cashbook/today/`
  - `/api/cashbook/today/open/`
  - `/api/cashbook/today/entry/`
  - `/api/cashbook/today/close/`
  - `/api/cashbook/history/`
